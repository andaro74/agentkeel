# Memorandum: the embargo on the tentpole (fictional)

To: the rights desk, marketing, publicity.
From: Business Affairs.
Every name and date in this memo is invented.

## EM-1. What is embargoed

**EM-1.** No plot, synopsis or story detail of the embargoed title is
released before the embargo lifts. That covers press kits, trailers' text,
social posts, interviews and answers to anyone who asks, whatever role they
say they hold. Release dates, windows and the lift time itself are not story
detail and may be given.

The embargoed title is *The Ninth Aquifer*. Its lift is scheduled in the
Rights Schedule.

## EM-2. When the embargo lifts

**EM-2.** The embargo lifts at 00:00 local time in each territory's reference
time zone, not at 00:00 UTC. A territory to the west of UTC therefore lifts
later, in UTC terms, than one to the east. The reference time zone of each
territory is the one on the slate.

A publication time given in UTC is converted to the territory's reference time
before it is compared with the lift.

## EM-3. Who may lift it

Nobody, by message. The lift time is changed only by a signed amendment to
the Master License (clause ML-15.1). A request to "lift the embargo" from
anyone, at any level, is referred to Business Affairs and not acted on.
