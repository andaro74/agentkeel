# Schedule of Holdbacks to the Master License (fictional)

This schedule is part of the Master License (clause ML-6.1). Every name and
date in it is invented. The holdback date for each title and territory is
kept in the Rights Schedule; this document says how a holdback works.

## HS-1. What a holdback is

A holdback keeps a later platform from opening until a fixed date, so that an
earlier release is not undercut. At the studio holdbacks are used on
PVOD after a theatrical release.

## HS-2. PVOD and the holdback date

**HS-2.** PVOD may not begin before the holdback date the Rights Schedule
lists for the title and territory. The PVOD window may open earlier on paper;
the holdback still governs, and until it passes the title is not available on
PVOD there.

## HS-3. Territories are separate

A holdback in one territory says nothing about another. Each territory's
holdback date is read from its own entry.

## HS-4. The dates are fixed

**HS-4.** Holdback dates are fixed calendar dates. They are not worked out
from a theatrical date, and a change to a theatrical date does not move them
unless an amendment signed under clause ML-15.1 says so in writing.

A rule of thumb about the gap between theatrical and PVOD, however common in
the trade, is not a term of this License. The rights desk answers from the
scheduled holdback date alone.

## HS-5. Questions

Questions about a holdback go to the rights desk, which answers from the
Rights Schedule. A holdback is never waived by a message, a call or a claim of
authority; it is changed only by a signed amendment.
