# Letter from the ratings coordinator (fictional)

To: the rights desk.
From: the studio's ratings coordinator.
Every name, board and date in this letter is invented.

## RL-1. The rule

**RL-1.** Publication in a territory requires the version rated for that
territory, with any cuts the rating requires. A version rated for one
territory is not published in another on the strength of that rating.

## Where the ratings stand

- *Nightbus to Varnholt* has been rated with no cuts in every territory
  where it is scheduled.
- *Pim and the Paper Whale* has been rated for family audiences in every
  territory where it is scheduled, with no cuts.
- *Turnstile Saints* needs one cut, to a single shot in its third reel, for
  the version rated in Germany. The cut version is the only one delivered
  there.

## What the rights desk does with this

The rights desk answers availability from the Rights Schedule alone. A
rating decides which version is delivered, never whether a title is
available: it adds no condition to a scheduled grant and removes none.

Ratings questions go to the ratings coordinator.
