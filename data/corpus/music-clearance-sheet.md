# Music Clearance Sheet (fictional)

This sheet is part of the Master License (clause ML-6.1). It records how
the music in the library titles is cleared. Every name, cue and date in it is
invented. The clearance expiry for each title and territory is kept in the
Rights Schedule.

## MC-1. Why the library needs this sheet

Older pictures were cleared for music territory by territory, for a term,
and some of those terms have run out. A title whose music is not cleared in a
territory cannot be published there, whatever its window says.

## MC-3. Expiry

**MC-3.** A title may not be published in a territory after its clearance
expiry until the clearances are renewed. The expiry date is the one the
Rights Schedule gives for the title and territory. A renewal is recorded by
the rights desk in the Rights Schedule, and only then does the title become
available again.

## MC-4. Territory by territory

**MC-4.** Music clearances are per territory. Expiry in one territory does
not affect another: a title can be cleared in one country and not in the
next.

## MC-5. Renewal

Renewals are negotiated by the music department with each publisher. Until a
renewal is signed and entered, the answer for that territory is that the
title is not available, with the clearance as the reason.
