# Master License (fictional)

Between the Licensor, the studio that holds the titles on the slate, and the
Licensee, the studio's own distribution arm. Every name, number and term in
this document is invented for agentkeel's reference agent.

## 1. Definitions

**Title.** A picture on the slate, identified by its title id (for example,
t-001). A title is one picture: a sequel, a prequel or a spin-off is a
title of its own.

**Rights Schedule.** The list of grants under this License: for each title,
territory and platform, the window, whether the grant is exclusive, and any
holdback, clearance expiry or embargo that applies. The Rights Schedule is
kept as a table by the Licensor's rights desk and is not reproduced in this
document. Where this License and the Rights Schedule disagree about a date or
a grant, the Rights Schedule governs.

**Platform.** One of THEATRICAL, PVOD (premium video on demand), SVOD
(subscription) and AVOD (advertising-supported).

**Territory.** A country, named by its two-letter code, with a reference
time zone for any time of day the Rights Schedule gives.

## 2. Grant of rights

**ML-2.1.** The Licensee may publish a title only in the territories and on
the platforms the Rights Schedule lists for that title. A territory or a
platform that is not listed is not granted, whatever any other document
says about the title.

**ML-2.2.** Nothing in this License is a grant by implication. A right that
is not scheduled has not been given.

**ML-2.3.** Sequels, prequels and spin-offs are separate titles. A grant for
one title conveys no rights in another, even where the two share a
franchise, a cast or a name. A sequel is published under its own scheduled
grants or not at all.

## 3. Term

**ML-3.1.** A title may be published only from the first day of its window
to the last day of its window, both days included, as the Rights Schedule
gives them. Before the window opens the title is not yet available; after
the window closes it is no longer available.

## 4. Delivery

**ML-4.1.** The Licensor delivers each title in the versions the ratings
provisions require (clause RL-1 in the ratings correspondence).

## 5. Exclusivity

**ML-5.1.** Exclusivity is scheduled, never assumed.

**ML-5.2.** Exclusivity is per title, territory and platform, as the Rights
Schedule gives it. A non-exclusive grant permits publication and supports no
exclusivity claim: the Licensee may publish, and may not describe the title
as available only on its service in that territory and on that platform.

## 6. Holdbacks, clearances and embargoes

**ML-6.1.** A holdback, a music-clearance expiry or an embargo in the
Rights Schedule limits a grant as the schedule to holdbacks, the music
clearance sheet and the embargo memorandum each say. Those documents are
part of this License.
